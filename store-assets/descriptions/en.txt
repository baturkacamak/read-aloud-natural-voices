Read Aloud: Natural Voices allows you to listen to web pages and documents aloud using Microsoft Edge's natural voices.

FEATURES
• Read the entire page, selected text, or from the cursor.
• Follow along with word and paragraph highlighting; adjust voice, speed, and pitch.
• Open PDFs, EPUBs, and images in the document reader; use OCR on scanned pages.
• Manage reading with bookmarks, reading list, timer, focus view, and offline recordings.
• Choose the interface language and use keyboard shortcuts.

HOW TO USE
1. Open a web page and click the extension icon.
2. Choose to read the entire page or selected text.
3. Pause, resume, or change speed from the player.

Internet is required for voice generation, and the text to be read is sent to Microsoft's voice service. Pre-prepared offline recordings can be listened to without internet. This independent extension is not affiliated with Microsoft.
