Read Aloud: Natural Voices ikuvumela ukuba ulalele amakhasi ewebhu namadokhumenti usebenzisa amazwi angokwemvelo.

IZICI
• Funda ikhasi lonke, umbhalo okhethiwe noma kusuka kusikhombi.
• Landela ukufunda ngokugqamisa amagama nezindima; lungisa izwi, isivinini nokuphakama kwezwi.
• Vula ama-PDF, ama-EPUB nezithombe kusifundi samadokhumenti; sebenzisa i-OCR emakhasini askenwe.
• Phatha ukufunda ngamabhukhimarki, uhlu lokufunda, isibali sikhathi, ukubukeka kokugxila kanye nokurekhodiwe okungaxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi.
• Khetha ulimi lwesikhombimsebenzisi futhi usebenzise izinqamuleli zekhibhodi.

INDLELA YOKUSEBENZISA
1. Vula ikhasi lewebhu bese uchofoza isithonjana sesandiso.
2. Khetha ukufunda ikhasi lonke noma umbhalo okhethiwe.
3. Misa kancane, qhubeka noma shintsha isivinini esidlalini.

Kudingeka i-inthanethi ukuze kukhiqizwe izwi futhi umbhalo ozofundwa uthunyelwa enkonzweni yezwi ye-Microsoft. Okurekhodiwe okungaxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi okulungiselelwe kusengaphambili kungalalelwa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi. Lesi sandiso esizimele asihlobene ne-Microsoft.
